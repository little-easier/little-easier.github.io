Draw a fish. Watch it swim.

Children draw anything they like, peel it off the page and drop it into their own pop-up aquarium, where it comes alive as a paper creature. They feed it, care for it and watch it grow, make friends and fall asleep at night.

Who it's for

Doodle Deep is rated 4+ and is in the App Store's Kids section for ages 6 to 8. That's where its drawing, caring and decorating feel just right for most children.

  • Younger children love tapping to colour in, feeding the fish and watching them swim. Some of the words (like "Peel me off!") are easier with a grown-up nearby.
  • Older children and grown-ups enjoy drawing their own creatures and decorations from scratch.
  • Children with SEND of any age: it was designed with them in mind. Nothing ever dies or is lost, there are no scores or ways to fail, and calm mode, a gentle session timer and simple words keep it predictable.
  • Grown-up areas (settings, sharing, printing and more time) are behind a check that asks for a number written in words, so children can't change them by accident.
  • There are no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no chat and no links out of the app.

Help and questions

Where are the grown-up settings?

Tap the ••• button in the top corner of the tank. You'll be asked to tap a number written in words, so only a grown-up can get in. Inside are the session timer, sound levels, calm mode, day and night, children's tanks, keepsakes and printable colouring pages.

How do I bring a paper drawing to life?

Tap Draw, then From a photo. Scan the drawing with the camera or pick a photo. Doodle Deep cuts it out on the device; you can use the "Keep more / Cut more" slider if it keeps too much or too little.

Can brothers and sisters have their own tank?

Yes. In the grown-ups area, open Children and add each child. Doodle Deep asks who's playing when it opens.

Will we lose our creatures?

Creatures never die. Everything is stored on the device, so it's included in your normal iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes its drawings, so please back up your device.

How do I remove a decoration?

Tap Decorate, then tap the decoration in the tank and tap the bin. To remove one of your own drawings from the tray, press and hold its tile.
