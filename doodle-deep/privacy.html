Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Doodle Deep lets children draw creatures that come alive in their own tank. This page explains what happens to their drawings and anything else they make.

The short version

  • We collect nothing. No accounts, no adverts, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything stays on your device. We never see it.
  • Nothing is shared unless a grown-up chooses to share it.
  • Photos of paper drawings are turned into creatures on the device itself.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything is kept inside the app's own storage on your device:

  • drawings, creatures, decorations and their names, birthdays and the "made by" name you type
  • the drawing journal, the recording of how each drawing was made (for the "watch me being drawn" film), photos taken with the in-app photo button, and treasures found
  • children's first names and profile pictures if you add more than one child
  • settings, such as sound, calm mode and the session timer

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers that the app talks to, and the app has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We can't see anything you or your child make or type in the app.

Doodle Deep works fully offline. It doesn't connect to the internet.

Permissions on your device

  • Camera: only if you choose to scan a drawing made on paper. The picture is turned into a creature on the device; the photo itself is not sent anywhere.
  • Photos: only if you choose a picture from your library to turn into a creature, or if a grown-up saves a photo, card, film or PDF to the library.

You can turn these off at any time in your device's Settings. The rest of the app works without them.

Sharing

Nothing leaves the device unless you choose to send it. When you press a share or save button, your device's own share sheet opens and you choose where it goes (for example Messages, Mail, Files or Photos). After that, it is handled by the app or service you picked.

Backups and deleting

If your device is backed up with iCloud or to a computer, Apple may include the app's data in that backup. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy, and we can't see it.

To delete everything, delete Doodle Deep from the device. Because we never hold a copy, we can't get anything back for you afterwards.

Children

Little Easier apps are made for children and the grown-ups who look after them. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no adverts, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links that take a child out of the app without a grown-up.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version of the app is released.
