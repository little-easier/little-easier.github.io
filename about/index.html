Help that shouldn't be locked away

Little Easier makes small, calm apps for children and the people who look after them, with SEND families in mind.

Why it exists

I've seen up close how much families of children with additional needs have to navigate: a world that isn't designed around their children, and isn't always as accommodating as it should be.

Good tools can genuinely help. But too often they're hidden behind adverts, subscriptions or apps that quietly collect data. Little Easier is a way to put that help within reach of every family, so that every child gets the same chance to be supported.

It isn't about making a profit. It's about making things a little easier.

Our promises

  • No adverts. Ever.
  • No paywalls on what matters. Everything that's free stays free.
  • No accounts and no tracking. Anything you add stays private on your phone or tablet. We never see it.
  • Works offline. No WiFi needed.
  • Calm and kind by design. Simple words, gentle sounds, nothing to fail, and grown-up areas kept safe behind a grown-up check.

The apps

Doodle Deep is the first on the App Store: children draw creatures that come alive in their own pop-up aquarium. It began with watching how much drawing can mean to a child who finds other ways of expressing themselves hard.

Calm Spaces, Timer, Feelings and Maths are next, all built the same way.
